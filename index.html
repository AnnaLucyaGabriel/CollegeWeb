About the Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering prepares students for careers in software development, data science, cybersecurity and research. The curriculum balances strong theoretical foundations with hands-on laboratory and project work.

Our vision is to produce graduates who are technically skilled, ethically grounded and ready to solve real-world problems. H2O may not be our subject, but x2 + y2 = z2 certainly appears in our algorithms classes!

"Programs must be written for people to read, and only incidentally for machines to execute."
— Harold Abelson
